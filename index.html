Iron Making

An Android app for blast furnace and iron making process calculations

About the app

Iron Making is an Android app for steel plant engineers, operators and students. It provides process calculations, models and reference data for the main iron making units:

  • Blast Furnace (BF) – burden, hot metal and process parameter calculations
  • Sinter Plant – sinter mix and quality calculations
  • Coke Oven – coke making calculations
  • Pellet Plant – pellet making calculations
