Privacy Policy – Iron Making

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Iron Making Android app ("the app"), developed by Niteen Kumar, handles your information.

1. Information we collect

When you sign in with Google, the app receives your basic Google profile information: your name, email address and profile picture. The app does not request access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts or other Google data.

Calculation inputs you enter in the app are used to produce results on your device.

2. How we use information

  • To sign you in and identify your account in the app
  • To provide and improve the app's features

3. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with third parties, except where required by law.

4. Google user data

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Data retention and deletion

You can sign out at any time. To ask for your data to be deleted, email us at the address below and we will delete it within 30 days.

6. Children

The app is meant for professionals and students in the steel industry and is not directed at children under 13.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
